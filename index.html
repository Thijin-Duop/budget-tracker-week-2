My Budget Tracker

My Budget Tracker is a simple website that helps you manage your money by recording your income and expenses.
It helps you organize your spending, understand your financial habits, and make better budgeting decisions.

How to use this tracker

Enter the name of your expense, the amount, select a category, and choose the date. Then click the Add Expense button. The expense information can later be connected to JavaScript to make the tracker fully interactive.
